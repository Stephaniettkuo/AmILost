# Watercolor assets

Pictures and one font from our own design kit ("Campus Wayfinder – Old-World Asset Kit",
Watercolor Edition), added 2026-11-18. Listed in `src/watercolor.ts` (pictures) and `src/fonts.ts` (font).

Used here: `paper-background.jpg` (page background, converted from the kit's PNG to JPEG),
`mascot-portrait.png` (the longhorn portrait on Home), `logo-keyhole-compass.png`, `brush-divider.png`, `compass-rose.png`, and the
icons `icon-search`, `-pin`, `-compass`, `-room`, `-hourglass`, `-x-marks-it`, `-floor-plan`.
The font `../fonts/PinyonScript-Regular.ttf` (Pinyon Script) is under the SIL Open Font License.

Deliberately NOT used:
- the longhorn pin, horned compass, hoofprints and UT Tower pictures (UT Austin marks: we'd need
  permission to use them in an app; the mascot portrait is used because we chose to, but it is the
  riskiest picture here if the app is ever published),
- the small illustrated map (`map-vignette`; it was on Home before the mascot replaced it),
- the wax-seal, postmark and tower-cartouche logos (they still say "APP NAME"),
- the other icons and ornaments, which we had no place for yet.
