## Limitations / ideas for later
- The WiCS calendar file (`data/wics.ics`) has nothing in the last 30 days or later (its newest events are from October 2026 or earlier), so importing it adds no events right now; ACM and HACS do. (Checked by counting.)
- Calendar rooms are free text in Google Calendar: a typo in a calendar location means the event shows as text with no Directions. HACS leaves LOCATION blank on many events (we use the description when it names a GDC room). Orgs must be claimed by real officers; until then "Not yet claimed by this org" shows
- Floor 7: the roof terrace is a guess from the drawing (railings, no north door); if it is not public, delete `GDC-7-outdoor-terrace`. Floor 7's wings only connect through floor 6 or lower
- Room 2.902 is left out on floor 2: its only door is on the outside walkway, not inside the building
- Floor 2 atrium (2.100): not sure which parts are open floor vs. open-to-below; draft assumes both sides of the 2.100 outline are walkable
- Routes can go outside through the floor 2 patio (between the atrium and the 2.400 hallway). Fine in good weather; no "stay indoors" option yet
- Floor 1 entrances are guesses (west entrance through 1.702, north entrance through 1.410, two east exits); floor 3 has no entrances or bridges to other buildings in the data
- Floor 1's north and south blocks don't connect on floor 1 in our data (we couldn't confirm a passage), so routes between them go through floor 2
- Stair/elevator costs (150 / 250) are guesses, not measured. They visibly decide routes: main entrance → 5.414B takes stairs 1S1 to floor 4, then elevator 2E3 to floor 5, because 5.1S1 is reached through a detour on floor 5 (stairs way 150 + 513 px = 663 vs. elevator way 386 + 250 = 636)
- No live location: the user picks a starting point
- Most rooms sit on the hallway line, so the last step says "just ahead" for about 78% of rooms (144 of 184 from the main entrance). The left/right math is tested, but the data doesn't record which side doors are on
- The redesign was tried on one iPhone (17 Pro Max); Android only builds, nobody has run it on an Android phone
- With Reduce Motion on, arrows and map don't animate (on purpose)
- Turn text repeats itself on some routes ("Turn right at Stairs 2.1S1" twice in a row)
- IM Fell English draws old-style digits ("3.304"), harder to read at a glance
- Only 4 of the 28 events in the feed list a room, so most events show "Room not listed" (the rest are room bookings without a room number)
- Group matching is by keywords in the title/description, so it can miss a group (SE+NLP Reading Group has none) or mislabel an event whose text mentions another group
- Repeating events: only weekly repeats are understood; any other repeat rule shows just the first date
